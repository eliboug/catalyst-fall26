**Thanks for coming to the Final Sprint kickoff!** We kicked off with the OpenAI Student Collective at Yale, handed out OpenAI subscriptions and formed teams. This sprint is open to everyone in y/cs, not only Catalyst members.

**The challenge:** build a tool for Yale students. Solve a problem you actually hit at Yale: planning your schedule, dining, finding study partners, club and event logistics, getting around campus. CourseTable, YaleMeals and Yalies all started as student projects. Other ideas are welcome, but message a lead to get them approved first. Small and useful beats big and unfinished.

**Teams:** about 4 people, your choice. Still solo? Message us in the GroupMe and we'll match you.

**Proposed timeline:**

- **Fri, Nov 20:** Pick your idea
- **Sun, Nov 22:** Working site with user log-in, plus a plan for how you'll handle user data
- **Nov 23 – 29:** Thanksgiving break, no work expected
- **Mon, Nov 30:** Start building features
- **Wed, Dec 2:** A working product to test at the workshop with the OpenAI team
- **Sun, Dec 6, 6–9pm:** Demo Day

**Points:** 2 for submitting your project, plus bonus points for winning. Post group photos for more.
